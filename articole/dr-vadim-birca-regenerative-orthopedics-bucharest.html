Most Requested

PRP Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma uses the healing growth factors in your own blood to reduce inflammation and stimulate cartilage repair. The gold standard for knee, hip and shoulder osteoarthritis.

  • 30-minute outpatient procedure
  • 1–3 sessions per protocol
  • Results visible within 2–4 weeks
  • Effect duration: 12–18 months
  • Ultrasound-guided precision injection
02
🔬
Advanced · Next Generation

Exosome Therapy

Exosomes are nanoscale vesicles that carry molecular instructions directly into damaged cells — reprogramming inflammation and activating deep tissue regeneration beyond what PRP alone can achieve.

  • Deeper biological regeneration than PRP
  • Ideal for moderate-to-advanced OA
  • Often combined with PRP (stacked protocol)
  • Effect duration: 12–24 months estimated
  • No rejection risk — biocompatible
03
💧
Viscosupplementation

Hyaluronic Acid Injections

Restores the lubricating properties of joint fluid, reducing friction and pain. Most effective when combined with PRP for patients with moderate osteoarthritis seeking longer-term comfort.

  • Immediate reduction in joint friction
  • Often combined with PRP for synergy
  • Particularly effective for knee & hip OA
  • Effect duration: 6–12 months
  • Well-established European clinical protocol
04
🏃
Integrated Protocol

Combined Protocol + Kinesitherapy

The most advanced approach: PRP prepares the joint environment, exosomes stimulate deep regeneration, and structured kinesitherapy ensures lasting functional improvement.

  • Session 1: PRP injection
  • Session 2 (7–14 days): exosome injection
  • 3–5 kinesitherapy sessions
  • Online follow-up at 3 and 6 months
  • Highest rate of long-term satisfaction

The Science

What Is PRP and How Does It Regenerate Your Knee?

PRP therapy uses the healing power already present in your own blood — concentrated and delivered precisely where your joint needs it most.

Platelets are not just involved in blood clotting — they carry powerful bioactive molecules that instruct your body to repair damaged tissue. Unlike cortisone injections, which only mask pain temporarily, PRP addresses the underlying degeneration by stimulating cartilage cells (chondrocytes), reducing chronic inflammation at the cellular level, and promoting the regeneration of ligament and tendon fibres. The procedure uses exclusively your own blood — no foreign substances, no risk of allergic reaction.

Ultrasound guidance matters. Dr. Bîrca performs all PRP injections under real-time ultrasound imaging. Studies show non-guided knee injections miss the joint space in up to 30% of cases. Ultrasound guidance is included in the €400 fee — it is not an optional add-on.
Patient Selection

Who Gets the Best Results from PRP?

PRP is most effective for patients in the early-to-moderate stages of joint degeneration who want to avoid or delay surgery.

🦵
Knee Osteoarthritis (Grade I–III)
Gonarthrosis at early to moderate stage — the most common indication. Not recommended for Grade IV bone-on-bone.
🔄
Meniscus Degeneration
Degenerative meniscus tears and post-meniscectomy recovery benefit significantly from PRP protocols.
⚡
Patellar & Quadriceps Tendinitis
Chronic tendon pain that hasn't responded to physiotherapy — PRP regenerates the tendon fibres directly.
🏃
Ligament Injuries & Sports Damage
ACL and MCL injuries in post-surgical recovery, as well as active sports patients wanting to avoid surgery.
🟡
Chondromalacia
Cartilage softening beneath the kneecap — common in active patients aged 30–55.
❌
Not Recommended For
Grade IV arthritis (bone-on-bone), active joint infection, anticoagulant therapy, certain blood disorders. A pre-consultation is always required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything International Patients Ask

Is PRP treatment safe in Romania?
+
Yes. Private clinics in Romania operate under EU healthcare regulations and hygiene standards. The key safety criteria are the same regardless of country: a certified orthopaedic specialist, ultrasound guidance for accurate placement, and sterile single-use equipment. Dr. Bîrca uses EU-grade certified PRP kits and performs all injections under real-time ultrasound imaging.
How many sessions will I need?
+
Most protocols involve 1–3 injections spaced 2–4 weeks apart. For moderate osteoarthritis, a 3-session protocol (€1,200 total) is typically recommended. The exact number is confirmed after reviewing your imaging during the pre-consultation. Some patients achieve excellent results with a single injection for tendon conditions.
Why is the price €400 in Bucharest when the UK charges €700–800?
+
The difference reflects the lower cost of living and operating costs in Romania — not a difference in medical quality or technology. The PRP preparation kits, centrifuge technology, ultrasound equipment, and Dr. Bîrca's training are equivalent to Western European standards. You receive the same procedure, with the same equipment, at a price determined by the Romanian healthcare market.
Can I fly the day after the procedure?
+
It is recommended to wait at least 24–48 hours before flying after a PRP injection. This allows the initial inflammatory response to settle. Most international patients plan a 4–5 day stay: Day 1 for consultation, Day 2 for the procedure, Day 3 for rest and light walking, Day 4 for physiotherapy, and Day 5 for departure.
Do I need to send my MRI before travelling?
+
Yes — and this is strongly advised. Never travel for a PRP injection without a confirmed pre-consultation. Dr. Bîrca's team will review your imaging, confirm your suitability for treatment, and outline the protocol before you book flights. This protects you from unnecessary travel and ensures your stay is fully productive.
Will my insurance cover PRP treatment abroad?
+
Standard travel insurance does not cover elective medical procedures. Some German and Austrian private health insurance policies offer partial reimbursement for PRP therapy — check with your insurer before booking. UK patients on private insurance should also verify their policy terms. We can provide a detailed treatment invoice to support any reimbursement claim.
What is the difference between PRP and exosome therapy?
+
PRP uses growth factors from your own blood to stimulate the body's healing response. Exosomes are nano-sized vesicles derived from stem cells that carry genetic instructions directly into the target cells, triggering a deeper regenerative process. For moderate osteoarthritis, PRP is the well-established first-line option. Exosome therapy may be discussed for more advanced cases or as a combined protocol. Ask about availability during your pre-consultation.
